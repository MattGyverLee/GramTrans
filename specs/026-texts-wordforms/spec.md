# Feature Specification: Texts & Wordforms (STUB)

**Feature Branch**: `026-texts-wordforms`

**Created**: 2026-07-11

**Status**: Stub / Planned (not yet specified)

**Depends on**: `024-lexicon-reference-fidelity` (fidelity guarantee, referenced-item
resolver) and likely `025-full-reversals` for ordering, though not a hard dependency.

## Purpose

Extend cross-project copy to **interlinear texts** and **wordform analyses** — the content
explicitly held out of features 024 and 025.

## Key Scope Decision (locked by user, 2026-07-11)

**Only HUMAN-evaluated wordform analyses are copied. Machine (parser) analyses are
ephemeral and MUST NOT be transferred.**

- A wordform analysis is in scope **only if it carries a human evaluation** — i.e. a human
  agent has **approved** (accepted) *or* **disapproved/denied** it. Both approved and
  denied human decisions are kept, because a human "no" is real curated data.
- Analyses with only machine/parser-agent evaluations (and no human decision) are treated
  as ephemeral: they are regenerated by the parser in the target and MUST be skipped.
- Rationale: parser output is reproducible noise; human approve/deny decisions are the
  irreplaceable curation the transfer must preserve.

## Intended Scope (to be refined in /speckit-specify)

- Interlinear **texts** and their segments/analyses.
- **Wordforms** and their analyses, filtered to the human-evaluated set above.
- Human agent evaluations (approved + disapproved) preserved on the copied analyses.
- The **never-silent guarantee** from 024 carries over.

## Out of Scope

- Machine/parser-only analyses (ephemeral — see locked decision).
- Anything already covered by 024 (lexical entries/senses/allomorphs) or 025 (reversals).

## Open Questions (for /speckit-specify)

- Does a human-approved analysis drag in its referenced senses/MSAs if those weren't
  already copied by 024, or is it skipped when its lexical targets are absent?
- How to represent a copied human "disapproved" evaluation when the target parser has not
  (yet) produced the matching candidate analysis.
- Text-level list references (genres, text markup tags) — resolve via the 024 resolver or
  defer.
